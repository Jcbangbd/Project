### NightPortHero 生成提示

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: opaque cinematic night-port hero background raster for a Unity digital-twin interface; background art only.
Input images: Image 1 is a composition, palette, realism, and mood reference. It contains a full UI; use ONLY the port scene in its central hero band below the navigation and above the nine-step strip as visual guidance. Do not reproduce any UI.
Primary request: Generate ONE crisp, high-quality realistic automated container-terminal panorama at night, matching the port-scene framing and professional industrial atmosphere of the reference, with no text or interface.
Scene/backdrop: deep petrol-blue night sky, harbor water at left, a container ship beside the quay, realistic ship-to-shore quay cranes, paved automated-vehicle lanes, and rows of gantry cranes and stacked containers receding far right.
Subject: a large realistic quay crane and container ship in the center-right, one physically plausible container-carrying AGV in the center-right foreground, gantry rows far right. Real steel structure, believable machinery and scale, containers with blank unbranded surfaces.
Style/medium: high-fidelity photorealistic professional digital-twin environment art; cinematic industrial realism, sharply resolved details, polished but physically grounded.
Composition/framing: ultrawide 8:3 panorama, target 1920x720 pixels. Ground-level wide establishing view looking down the quay with perspective converging toward the right. Keep the left 30% as clean dark negative space, a natural deep-sky/water fade with very low detail and no focal object; preserve room there for a separately editable Chinese heading and paragraph. Main port machinery begins near the center and occupies the right two-thirds. The ship bow and lit quay structure may approach the center but must not intrude into the left negative space. Make the large crane feel monumental and the AGV recognizable, as in the reference hero crop.
Lighting/mood: restrained icy-cyan rim lighting on blue steel, tiny warm practical port lamps, subtle reflections on harbor water and lightly wet pavement, calm competent night-operation atmosphere. Limited atmosphere haze only for depth, no heavy fog.
Color palette: deep petrol teal #071E28 and navy, subdued steel blue, restrained pale icy cyan highlights, small warm amber-white lamps.
Constraints: opaque full-bleed background. NO UI, NO text, NO headings, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos, NO symbols, NO borders, NO frames, NO watermarks. No baked gradient panels, no navigation, no buttons, no workflow strip. Avoid cartoon treatment, science fiction, excessive neon, exaggerated bloom, excessive fog, people, legible container branding, diagram overlays.

### DischargeBlueprintStrip 最终修订提示

Refine the attached image into clean monochrome blueprint OUTLINE line art on a genuinely transparent background. Keep all NINE existing drawings, equipment details, order, one horizontal row, diagram positions, object proportions and transparent upper/lower padding exactly as they are.

The sole change is line treatment: remove every cyan/turquoise filled area, colored halo, white fringe, grain and glow. Retain ONLY the fine precise outlines and internal engineering lines, redrawn in solid pale OFF-WHITE #D6EEF3. Do not tint cyan; monochrome off-white strokes only, no color fill anywhere. Every space inside machinery, vehicles, containers, racks and platforms must be fully transparent except for the thin technical outlines. The source depicts nine outline drawings, not filled blue silhouettes; make that distinction clean and clear. Sharp 1–2 px uniform strokes. Background and object face interiors must remain completely transparent except for the thin outlines. No haze or painted background.

No words, letters, numbers, logos, symbols, arrows, border, UI, ground line or labels. Do not introduce anything. Preserve all nine machinery drawings and make their fine off-white line art clean and legible.
